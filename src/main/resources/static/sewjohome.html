Frequently asked questions

Is there an app I can download?

Sewjo is a web application that you can use on any device with a browser! We do not have iOS or Android apps (so you won’t find us in any app store), but you can add Sewjo to the home screen on your phone for an app-like experience.

Is Sewjo free?

Yes, Sewjo is free to use. We don’t have any limits on the number of fabrics or patterns you can add to your stash, neither do we limit how many sewing projects you can plan and share with the community. We do offer a plus subscription for those who want to support the platform and get access to advanced features and stats.
